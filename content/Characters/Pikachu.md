## vs [[Ness]]
- Don't be under side plat when ness is above you

### How to get in

- Landing fair to grab is really good against ness

### Edgeguarding
- It can be surprisingly hard to edge guard a ness recovering close with a jump. Short hop up air and down tilt work best.
- Good nesses recovering low aim use pk thunder backwards (clockwise if on the left) and try to hit you with the tail of pk thunder, comboing into pk thunder 2.
